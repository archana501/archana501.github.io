Project Overview

What this dashboard analyzes and how it supports decisions.

About the project

This project provides a consolidated view of Nykaa's sales performance across products, categories, customers, and regions. Interactive visualizations help explore revenue trends, profitability, customer behavior, and return patterns to support business decision-making.

Project goals

  • Analyze overall sales, profit, and order performance.
  • Identify top-performing products and categories.
  • Evaluate profit margins and product return patterns.
  • Understand customer demographics and regional sales trends.
  • Identify opportunities to improve business profitability.

Dashboard Pages

01 — Sales & Product Analysis

This page provides an overview of sales performance through total sales, profit, orders, and returns. Monthly trends, product sales, and category performance help identify leading revenue contributors, while interactive filters support detailed exploration.

02 — Profit & Return Insights

This page focuses on profitability, profit margins, and product return performance to help identify areas for business improvement. It compares sales and profit across categories, examines return distribution, and highlights the main reasons for returns.

03 — Customer & Regional Insights

This page analyzes customer demographics and regional sales performance across cities, genders, and age groups. Interactive filters allow users to explore customer segments and understand variations in sales across locations.

Key Business Insights

Findings reported from the analysis.

The business generated approximately ₹5M+ in total sales and ₹1.15M in total profit.

The overall profit margin was 22.3%.

Skincare was the highest-performing category, generating approximately ₹2.6M in sales.

Mumbai was the highest revenue-generating city among the analyzed locations.

Customers aged 18–25 contributed the highest sales.

Wrong Item and Damaged are leading return reasons to investigate.

Female customers contribute a larger share of sales than male customers in this dataset.

The overall return rate was 10%, with 200total returns.

Business Conclusion

The Nykaa Sales Analysis Dashboard provides visibility into sales, profitability, product returns, customer demographics, and regional performance. Skincare and Mumbai emerged as key sales contributors, while customers aged 18–25 represented an important segment. Return analysis highlighted areas that may require operational attention. Overall, the dashboard supports performance monitoring and informed business decisions.
