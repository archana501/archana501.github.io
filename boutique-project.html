Project Overview

What this dashboard analyzes and how it supports decisions.

About the project

This project transforms boutique transactional sales data into meaningful business insights through an interactive Excel dashboard. It helps evaluate sales trends, profitable categories, customer purchasing patterns, best-selling products, and key retail performance indicators.

Project goals

  • Analyze overall sales, profit, and order performance.
  • Identify best-selling products and profitable categories.
  • Understand customer spending and purchase frequency.
  • Evaluate discount performance and payment preferences.
  • Compare sales across months, genders, and salespeople.

Tools & technologies

Tools and concepts used in the project:

Microsoft ExcelPivot TablesPivot Charts Excel FormulasData Visualization KPI DesignData Analysis

Dashboard features

  • Interactive slicers and filters.
  • KPI cards for sales, profit, and customer metrics.
  • Monthly sales trend analysis.
  • Category and product performance comparison.
  • Customer behavior and demographic analysis.
  • Payment method and gender-wise sales analysis.
  • Discount and profitability analysis.

Dashboard Pages

01 — Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard provides a consolidated overview of sales, profit, orders, customers, and average order value. It visualizes monthly sales, category performance, payment preferences, gender-wise sales, and salesperson contribution.

02 — Sales Analysis

The Sales Analysis page focuses on product sales, profitability, discount performance, and category comparisons. It helps identify high-performing products and examine sales and profit trends across different months and discount levels.

03 — Customer Analysis

The Customer Analysis page explores customer spending, purchase frequency, age groups, and city-wise distribution. It identifies frequent and high-value customers to provide a clearer understanding of customer purchasing patterns.

04 — Product Analysis

The Product Analysis page examines product performance through quantity sold, category-wise quantities, product costs, and profitability. It helps identify best-selling products and categories that contribute strongly to sales.

Key Business Insights

Findings reported from the analysis.

The boutique generated approximately ₹52.98 lakh in sales and ₹21.68 lakh in profit.

The overall profit margin was 40.92%.

November recorded the highest monthly sales.

Ethnic Wear contributed the highest profit, while Lehenga generated the highest product sales.

Denim Jeans was the best-selling product by quantity.

Footwear and Accessories recorded the highest quantity sold among categories.

Kavya Iyer was the most frequent customer, while Arjun Nair generated the highest customer sales.

Customers aged 26–35 formed the largest age group; female customers contributed more sales, Debit Card was the preferred payment method, and discounts showed no consistent relationship with sales.

Business Conclusion

The Boutique Sales Analysis Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of retail performance, profitability, customer behavior, and product trends. The analysis identifies November as the strongest sales month and highlights leading products and categories. Customer insights reveal valuable segments and purchasing patterns. Overall, the project demonstrates how Excel dashboards can transform transactional data into practical insights for retail decision-making.
