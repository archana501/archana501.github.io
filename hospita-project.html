Project Overview

What this dashboard analyzes and how it supports decisions.

About the project

This project transforms hospital data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations and predictive analytics. It helps stakeholders monitor hospital performance, evaluate healthcare operations, analyze patient risk, and understand future trends.

Project goals

  • Analyze patient admissions, hospital revenue, and operational performance.
  • Monitor bed occupancy and department performance.
  • Evaluate doctor performance and patient activity.
  • Classify patients according to predicted risk levels.
  • Forecast patient volume, revenue, admissions, and bed occupancy.

Dashboard Pages

01 — Home

The Home page provides an interactive navigation interface for accessing the Overview, Healthcare, Doctors, Prediction, and Forecast sections. It offers a centralized entry point for exploring different aspects of hospital performance.

02 — Overview

The Overview page summarizes overall hospital performance through patient, revenue, doctor, and admission metrics. Its visualizations highlight monthly trends, patient distribution, department activity, gender, and bed types.

03 — Healthcare Analysis

The Healthcare page focuses on patient admissions, bed occupancy, department performance, and average length of stay. It helps stakeholders monitor hospital capacity, departmental revenue, and seasonal admission patterns.

04 — Doctors Analysis

The Doctors page compares doctors based on patients treated, revenue contribution, ratings, and consultation fees. It provides insights into doctor performance and differences across departments.

05 — Patient Risk Prediction

The Prediction page uses machine learning-based analysis to classify patients into High, Medium, and Low Risk categories. It explores risk distribution, high-risk diseases, department patterns, and admission types to support patient risk monitoring.

06 — Forecast

The Forecast page presents expected patient volume, admissions, revenue, and occupied beds for the next three months. It supports forward-looking analysis and hospital resource planning.

Key Business Insights

Findings reported from the analysis.

Bed occupancy monitoring helps evaluate hospital capacity and resource utilization.

Machine learning-based analysis classifies patients into High, Medium, and Low Risk groups.

High-risk patient analysis highlights disease categories and departments that may require closer attention.

Interactive filters help stakeholders explore hospital performance and support data-driven decisions.

Business Conclusion

The Hospital Management Analytics Dashboard combines operational reporting, doctor performance analysis, patient risk prediction, and forecasting. Overall, the project demonstrates how Power BI and machine learning can support informed healthcare management decisions.
